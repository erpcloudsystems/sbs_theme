Family Home Experience Managers

Butler introduces a group of experience managers, each one designed to serve a specific purpose.

based on what are the “Family Home Living Values” [Comfort, Efficiency, Security, Entertainment, & Hospitality.

Pictured from left, their names are :
  • Mr.Security:Complicate home access for thief & simplify home access for family.
  • Mr.Efficient: Optimizes lighting and energy used in high-use home areas.
  • Mr.Alfred: Ensures bedrooms areas always feel comfort.
  • Mrs.Hospitality: Prepares guest area and welcomes visitors with elegance.
  • Mr.Entertainment: Manages leisure areas like cinemas, gyms, and swimming pools.
